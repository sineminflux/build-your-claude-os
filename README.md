# Build Your Own Claude OS

A plugin that sets up a workspace your business runs from, so Claude knows your business, your voice, and how you work, every time you open it. Built for non-technical founders. You answer a short interview and Claude builds every file for you.

## What you get

Installing the plugin adds four skills:

- **Claude OS Builder.** A guided setup that stands up your workspace: a Front Desk router (`CLAUDE.md`), a `MEMORY.md`, a brand one-pager, your voice guidelines, and your own departments, wired to your tools. Say "build my Claude OS" to start.
- **Voice Profile Builder.** Learns how you write from your real samples and saves it as your voice, so Claude drafts as you.
- **No AI Slop.** Writing rules that kill the tells of unedited AI copy (em dashes, "it's not X, it's Y" pivots, throat-clearing openers, filler). Runs on everything Claude writes for you.
- **Session Audit.** At the end of a work session, say "audit this session" and it saves what you decided or corrected into your memory files, and creates any tasks that came up in your task system.

## How your OS works once it is built

Claude reads your Front Desk and memory at the start of every session, so it always knows your business. Writing loads your voice and the anti-slop rules automatically. Your memory grows as you work, and the OS expands by opening new departments as your business does. Nothing gets sent, posted, or deleted without your okay.

## Install it

See [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md). The short version: in the Claude desktop app, open Cowork, go to Customize, Plugins, add this marketplace by its link, and install the Claude OS plugin. Then open a workspace folder and say "build my Claude OS".
