# Build Your Own Claude OS

Set up a workspace your business runs from, so Claude knows your business, your voice, and how you work every time you open it. Built for non-technical founders: you answer a short interview and Claude builds every file for you.

## What it is

One plugin, six builders. You install it once, then run the builder you want. Each one builds a piece of your OS for you, so you never install or write a skill yourself.

The main builder stands up your workspace in one session:

- **Claude OS Builder** ("build my Claude OS"). Builds your folder: a Front Desk router (`CLAUDE.md`), a `MEMORY.md`, a brand one-pager, and your own departments. Your two everyday skills run automatically from the plugin, and the builder also hands you savable copies to own:
  - **No AI Slop** kills the tells of unedited AI copy (em dashes, "it's not X, it's Y" pivots, throat-clearing openers, filler) on everything Claude writes for you.
  - **Session Audit** ("audit this session") saves what you decided or corrected into your memory files, creates any tasks that came up, and keeps your OS lean. It also offers to schedule a recurring tidy.

The follow-up builders each build one deeper document from what you already have, run by command in a fresh session so no single session gets slow:

- **Voice, ICP, Design Guidelines, and Brand Story builders** ("build my voice", "build my ICP", "build my design guidelines", "build my brand story").
- **The Migrator** ("migrate my old folder", "bring in my old Project") brings your old material into the OS, whether it lives in an old folder, a workspace, or a Claude Project. It sorts every item into the right file, copies rather than moves, proposes before it writes, and leaves stale content behind.

## You own your skills

No AI Slop and the Session Audit run automatically because they ship in the plugin, so you are covered from the moment you install it. The builder also hands you your own savable copies during setup, so you own editable versions and never hunt for or upload them. Every builder drops its editable source into a top-level `_skills/` folder in your workspace, so the skills are yours: change one by asking Claude to edit its source and rebuild it, then click Save again. You can remove the plugin afterward and keep everything, any skill you saved keeps running and the sources stay in `_skills/`.

## How your OS works once it is built

Claude reads your Front Desk and memory at the start of every session, so it always knows your business. Writing loads the No AI Slop rules automatically, and your voice too once you have built it. Your memory grows as you work, the OS expands by opening new departments as your business does, and it stays lean because the Session Audit prunes as it writes. Nothing gets sent, posted, or deleted without your okay.

## Install it

See [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md). The short version: in the Claude desktop app, open Cowork, go to Customize, Plugins, add this marketplace by its GitHub link, and install the Claude OS plugin. Then open a workspace folder and say "build my Claude OS". No AI Slop and the Session Audit are already running from the plugin, and the builder hands you savable copies to keep; the deeper documents build in follow-up sessions, starting with "build my voice".
