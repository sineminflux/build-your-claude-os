# Build Your Own Claude OS

Set up a workspace your business runs from, so Claude knows your business, your voice, and how you work every time you open it. Built for non-technical founders: you answer a short interview and Claude builds every file for you.

## What it is

One plugin, five builders. You install it once, then run the builder you want. Each one builds a piece of your OS for you, so you never install or write a skill yourself.

The main builder stands up your workspace in one session:

- **Claude OS Builder** ("build my Claude OS"). Builds your folder: a Front Desk router (`CLAUDE.md`), a `MEMORY.md`, a brand one-pager, and your own departments. In the same session it also builds your two everyday skills and hands each to you to Save with one click:
  - **No AI Slop** kills the tells of unedited AI copy (em dashes, "it's not X, it's Y" pivots, throat-clearing openers, filler) on everything Claude writes for you.
  - **Session Audit** ("audit this session") saves what you decided or corrected into your memory files, creates any tasks that came up, and keeps your OS lean. It also offers to schedule a recurring tidy.

The follow-up builders each build one deeper document from what you already have, run by command in a fresh session so no single session gets slow:

- **Voice, ICP, Design Guidelines, and Brand Story builders** ("build my voice", "build my ICP", "build my design guidelines", "build my brand story").

## You own your skills

You save exactly two skills, No AI Slop and the Session Audit, and you never hunt for or upload them: the main builder assembles each one during setup and hands you the file, so all you do is click Save. Every builder also drops its editable source into a top-level `_skills/` folder in your workspace, so the skills are yours: change one by asking Claude to edit its source and rebuild it, then click Save again for the two saved ones. You can remove the plugin afterward and keep everything, the two saved skills keep running and the document-builder sources stay in `_skills/`.

## How your OS works once it is built

Claude reads your Front Desk and memory at the start of every session, so it always knows your business. Writing loads the No AI Slop rules automatically, and your voice too once you have built it. Your memory grows as you work, the OS expands by opening new departments as your business does, and it stays lean because the Session Audit prunes as it writes. Nothing gets sent, posted, or deleted without your okay.

## Install it

See [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md). The short version: in the Claude desktop app, open Cowork, go to Customize, Plugins, add this marketplace by its GitHub link, and install the Claude OS plugin. Then open a workspace folder and say "build my Claude OS". You Save your two everyday skills during that session; the deeper documents build in follow-up sessions, starting with "build my voice".
