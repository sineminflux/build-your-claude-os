# Starter Prompt (copy and paste)

Not technical? You don't have to run the phases yourself. Paste one of the prompts below into your AI (Claude Cowork, Claude Code, or a chat with this skill loaded) and follow along. It runs the whole build for you and asks you questions as it goes.

Pick the version that matches you.

---

## Version A: your email or docs are connected

Use this if you've connected Gmail, Google Drive, or Notion to your AI, so it can read your real writing.

```
I want you to build my voice profile using the voice-profile-builder skill.

Read a wide pile of my real writing first. Pull from whatever I've connected:
my sent emails (the ones I actually wrote, not quoted replies), my own docs,
my posts. Aim for at least 10 pieces across different moods and places. Only
my own writing, nothing drafted or polished by AI.

Then run the five phases: measure my mechanics with real numbers, extract my
patterns, and build me two files, voice-principles.md and voice-examples.md.
Show me the measured numbers as you go so I can see the evidence.

Before you finish, calibrate: draft two short things in my voice and let me
tell you what's off. Fold my corrections in as rules. Loop until both sound
like me.
```

---

## Version B: nothing connected, you'll paste samples

Use this if you'd rather paste your writing in by hand.

```
I want you to build my voice profile using the voice-profile-builder skill.

I'm going to paste samples of my own writing below: posts, emails, captions,
anything that sounds like me. All written by me, nothing AI-drafted. Include
my range, serious and casual, long and short.

Analyze them and run the five phases: measure my mechanics with real numbers,
extract my patterns, and build me two files, voice-principles.md and
voice-examples.md. Show me the numbers so I can see the evidence.

Then calibrate: draft two short things in my voice and let me tell you what's
off. Turn my corrections into rules. Loop until both sound like me.

Here are my samples:
[paste 8 to 15 pieces below, the more variety the better]
```

---

## What you'll get back

Two files:

- **voice-principles.md**: how your voice works. Keep this loaded whenever an AI writes as you (in a Claude Project, a skill folder, or pasted into a system prompt).
- **voice-examples.md**: an annotated bank of your real passages. Load it too when the writing is long-form.

## A few tips for better results

- Give it your everyday writing, not just your polished best-of. Sent emails and DMs often show your voice more honestly than a labored essay.
- If you can, use writing from before you leaned on AI. It reflects your own voice more cleanly.
- When it drafts something that isn't you, say exactly what's off. Every correction becomes a permanent rule, so the profile gets sharper every time you fix a line.
- Pair it with an anti-slop rule set. The voice file says what your voice is. Anti-slop rules catch the generic tells. Together they keep your drafts honest.
